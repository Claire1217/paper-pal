\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Light at night.} Early surveys compared a handful of city parks with nearby forest and found that urban birds sang between ten and ninety minutes earlier \citep{hartwell2009urban}. Later work measured illuminance directly and reported a graded relationship rather than a simple urban--rural difference \citep{okafor2015light}. Both lines of work treated noise as a nuisance variable and did not sample it.

\paragraph{Noise.} A separate literature argues that birds adjust the timing, pitch, and amplitude of song to escape masking by traffic \citep{lindqvist2012noise, moreau2018masking}. Most of this evidence comes from airports and motorways, where noise is extreme and lighting was not recorded.

\paragraph{Separating the two.} To our knowledge only \citet{tanaka2020gradient} sampled both variables at the same sites, and their 14 sites were too few to separate the effects. Our design follows theirs but triples the number of sites and selects them to break the correlation between light and noise.
